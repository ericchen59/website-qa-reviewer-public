## Performance Narrative Copy

**CT5-V paragraph**

> Right from the start, you’ll notice several key distinctions between these two high-octane luxury sedans. Standard power for the Cadillac CT5-V is a muscular 3.0L twin-turbocharged V6 that comes mated to a responsive 10-speed automatic transmission. In total, you’ll have access to an outstanding 360 horsepower and 405 lb-ft of torque. This gives the CT5-V the ability to accelerate from 0 to 60 MPH in a blistering 4.6 seconds. Balancing comfort with athleticism, the CT5-V also comes equipped with the legendary Magnetic Ride Control performance suspension that can adapt to the conditions within milliseconds.

**CT5-V Blackwing paragraph**

> Shifting focus to the adrenaline-pumping CT5-V Blackwing, you’ll notice that it’s powered by the purpose-built 6.2L supercharged V8 engine tuned specifically for Cadillac. Boasting significantly more power than its stablemate, the CT5-V Blackwing delivers 668 horsepower, 659 lb-ft of torque, and a remarkable 3.4-second 0-60 time. Along with the same 10-speed automatic that we mentioned above, another noteworthy feature unique to Blackwing variants is an available 6-speed manual transmission. It also features the same Magnetic Ride Control system with adaptive geometry settings that keeps the vehicle poised and planted at high speeds on the track.

## Key Features — Exterior

**Section heading**

> Exterior Features Compared

**Body copy**

> Although it’s quite easy to see the similarities between the two, the Cadillac CT5-V and CT5-V Blackwing do have some noteworthy differences when comparing their exterior designs. Looking at them both from the side, you’ll instantly notice their planted, aggressive stance on the road. Both vehicles feature 19-inch alloy wheel designs available in finishes that include polished silver, painted bronze, and painted graphite. A wide mesh grille also offers enhanced functionality by cooling the main radiators via dedicated airflow ducts. Blackwing variants can be identified by a number of exclusive exterior accents not found on the standard CT5-V. Highlights of such features include gloss black rocker moldings with extensions, a rear decklid spoiler, and a front splitter. Also available exclusively on CT5-V Blackwing models is an optional Carbon Fiber package that reduces weight while also adding a track-inspired look to the exterior.

## Key Features — Interior

**Section heading**

> Interior Amenities Compared

**Body copy**

> Despite sharing a virtually identical interior layout, there are still plenty of key differences between the cabin of the Cadillac CT5-V and CT5-V Blackwing. Examples of features found inside the Blackwing that you won’t find on the standard CT5-V include optional Torch Red seat belts, sueded-microfiber headliner, high-performance steering wheel with carbon fiber trim and serialized VIN plate, and carbon fiber accented front seats. Regardless of which model you select, you’ll enjoy state-of-the-art connectivity made possible by the standard 33-inch diagonal advanced color LED display with Google Built-In with over-the-air software updates. A standard Head-Up Display and Integrated Data Performance Recorder are two additional features exclusive to the Cadillac CT5-V and CT5-V Blackwing.
